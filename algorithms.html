How the algorithms work

This site doesn't just train an AI to play Tetris — it's meant to show you how different AI approaches actually work, using the same simple game as a common example. Pick an algorithm below.

Genetic Algorithm

Evolve a population of Tetris-playing strategies through selection, crossover, and mutation — survival of the fittest.

CMA-ES

Search for good strategies by adaptively reshaping a cloud of guesses toward whatever worked best.

NEAT

Evolve the shape of a small neural network itself — growing new connections and nodes only when they help.

Reinforcement Learning

No population at all — one agent that learns gradually from the reward of every single move it makes.
